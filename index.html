How it works

Bookmarklets are tiny programs that live in your bookmarks bar. When you click one, it runs a quick script in your browser.

A "lens" narrows your Google search to only show results from websites you trust. Click a lens in your bookmarks bar, type what you're looking for, and you'll get results from just those sites — no noise, no junk.

It's like having custom search engines for different parts of your life — recipes from your favorite food blogs, patterns from your go-to fiber arts sites, etc.

Made for Lydia :) with implementation inspiration from Kagi's search lenses — a similar feature built into their search engine. This is a lightweight, bookmark-based version that works with plain old Google.
